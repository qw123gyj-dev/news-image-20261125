CHINA — SHANGHAI

China probes its Shanghai party chief

Chen Jining, a Politburo member once tipped for the top, is the fourth of the body to fall.
China’s anti-graft watchdog said on Tuesday it is investigating Chen Jining, the Communist Party chief of Shanghai and a member of the elite Politburo, for suspected “severe violations of discipline and laws”. Chen, 62, who has run China’s financial hub since 2022, is the fourth member of the 24-strong Politburo to be probed since 2025, and the first Shanghai chief to fall since 2006. Shanghai’s leadership pledged loyalty to Beijing at a late-night meeting.
TECH — THE COURTS

Huawei convicted on 10 of 12 counts in US trial

Brooklyn jury finds fraud and trade-secrets conspiracy; racketeering charge fails.
A federal jury in Brooklyn convicted Huawei on Tuesday on 10 of 12 criminal counts, including bank and wire fraud, sanctions violations and conspiracy to steal trade secrets from US companies. Jurors acquitted the firm of racketeering conspiracy, the most serious charge. The case, brought in 2019, could bring billions in penalties when Huawei is sentenced next September.
WAR — THE BALTIC

Zelenskiy confirms Ukraine hit a Russian ship

Sparta II was a legitimate target carrying weapons, he says; Norway urges caution.
President Volodymyr Zelenskiy confirmed on Tuesday that Ukraine was behind Saturday’s drone strike on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland, calling the vessel a legitimate military target because it was carrying weapons. Norway, a strong supporter of Kyiv, urged caution in such operations.
MIDDLE EAST — RIYADH

Houthi drone hits Riyadh’s tallest tower

Eleven wounded in the second strike on the PIF tower in under a week.
A Houthi drone struck a tower in Riyadh’s King Abdullah Financial District on Tuesday, wounding 11 people, Saudi civil defence said. Witnesses said the building hit was the PIF tower, the capital’s tallest; it was struck once before, on 19 November, with no injuries.
AMERICA — WASHINGTON

Trump gives Giuliani the Medal of Freedom

The nation’s highest civilian honour, in a private Oval Office ceremony.
President Donald Trump awarded the Medal of Freedom, the nation’s highest civilian honour, to former New York mayor Rudy Giuliani on Tuesday. Trump tied the medal around Giuliani’s neck in a private Oval Office ceremony, 14 months after first promising him the award.
AMERICA — CYBER

CIA and ODNI hit by cyber breach

Unclassified data exposed; classified systems said to be untouched.
The CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence recently discovered a cybersecurity breach that exposed some of their unclassified information, the New York Times reported on Tuesday. The intrusion hit a network that mines public material; what was taken is unclear.
EUROPE — PARIS

Sarkozy and Bruni to stand trial

Witness-tampering case over the Libyan funding affair goes to court in 2027.
French judges ordered former president Nicolas Sarkozy and his wife Carla Bruni to stand trial on Tuesday over alleged witness tampering in the case about funding for his 2007 election campaign. The trial is set for November 2027; both deny wrongdoing and face up to 10 years.
